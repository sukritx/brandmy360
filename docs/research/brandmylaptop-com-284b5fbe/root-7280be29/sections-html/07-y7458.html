How it works

Most lids sell at a fixed price. Some run an auction instead: brands bid for a few days, and the highest approved bid wins at the close.

  1. 1

    A creator lists their lid

    Mac or PC, the spots and their prices, how long the stickers stay. $19 once, Stripe connected, and the lid is live at its own address.

  2. 2

    A brand picks a spot

    Or posts a brief and lets creators apply. The creator approves the logo by hand, and that is what takes the payment — straight into their own Stripe.

  3. 3

    Stickers on, photo posted

    The creator prints the stickers at the size each spot lists, puts them on, and posts the photo. The platform keeps 10% — 15% on an auction — and never touches the rest.
